Cross-border wire payments

Cross-border wire payments

Cross-border wire payments are coming soon

All information on this page describes a solution that is not yet live in production and therefore could change. This includes any endpoint and webhook details. The information is being provided early to help you integrate with our API in our EU simulation environment.

Our cross-border wire payments service lets you send and receive US dollar (USD) payments internationally.

Cross-border wire payments remain in the same currency from end to end - there's no foreign exchange or currency conversion involved. To send payments that change currency, use our Cross-border FX service.

If you're interested in making payments with this service, please speak with your Client Director.

Key service information

Payment limitations

  • Currencies supported: Only US dollars can be sent and received using this service. Payments must be made from a USD enabled multi-currency account.
  • Payment size: Outbound payments must be greater than 13.00 USD. This is specified in the instructedAmount field of the payment request.
  • Supported regions: Payments can be sent and received internationally, but are subject to specific country restrictions. For details of ClearBank's restricted regions, please refer to our service agreement and associated policies.

Service hours and holidays

Operating times: This service operates between 09:30 and 19:00 CET/CEST, Monday to Friday.

Holidays: The service does not operate on US and German (TBC) public holidays.

Outbound payments are not accepted outside of our service hours. If you request an outbound payment when this service is closed, the payment will not proceed and we'll respond to your request with a 422 - Unprocessable Entity response. You should try again when operating hours resume.

Inbound payments are processed regardless of our service hours. You should always expect to receive Inbound Payment Completed webhooks at all hours when using this service. If an inbound payment fails it will be handled manually within service hours.

Payment deduplication and uniqueness

Duplicated outbound payment requests are prevented via unique messageIdentification and uetr values:

  • If you reuse a messageIdentification in your request, you'll receive a 409 - Conflict response. We will treat the payment as a duplicate and it will not be processed.
  • If you reuse a uetr in your request, the payment will fail after being accepted and you'll be notified of this via webhook. We will treat the payment as a duplicate and it will not be processed.

Reasons for payment failure

Outbound payments may fail after we've accepted them for the following reasons*:

  • There are insufficient funds in your account.
  • Your account doesn't exist, isn't active, or can't hold USD.
  • The uetr or messageIdentification you provided for this payment has already been used in another payment.
  • Your payment has failed our screening checks.
  • Your payment was rejected further along the payment chain, such as by an intermediary bank.

If any of these failures occur, you'll receive a Cross-border Outbound Payment Failed webhook.

Inbound payments can only be credited to you if your account is a multi-currency account that is active and enabled for USD. You should ensure these criteria are met before attempting to receive inbound payments.

*International payments can fail for myriad reasons: the above reasons are not exhaustive.

How to send cross-border wire payments

Webhooks and endpoints

To send cross-border wire payments, you'll need to integrate with our POST /payments/cross-border/v2/payments endpoint and its associated webhooks:

  • Cross-border Outbound Payment Completed webhook
  • Cross-border Outbound Payment Failed webhook
  • Cross-border Outbound Payment Return Completed webhook
  • Customer Accounts Transaction Completed webhook - this is a generic webhook sent for all account debits and credits regardless of the service or rail used.

Accounts and configuration (Outbound)

Multi-currency accounts: This service requires you to have an active multi-currency account that's enabled for USD. This will be set up for you as part of your onboarding.

Webhook subscription: You must subscribe to this service's webhooks in the ClearBank Portal to receive updates about the status of your outbound payments.

Track outbound payments

When you successfully request a cross-border wire payment, you'll receive a 202 - Accepted response. This response contains several identifiers you can refer to when reconciling your payment with its associated webhooks:

  • instructionIdentification
  • endToEndIdentification
  • uetr
  • paymentId

How to receive cross-border wire payments

Webhooks

To receive notifications about inbound cross-border wire payments, you should integrate with two webhooks:

  • Cross-border Inbound Payment Completed webhook
  • Customer Accounts Transaction Completed webhook

There are no endpoints associated with receiving cross-border wire payments.

Accounts and configuration (Inbound)

Multi-currency accounts: To receive cross-border wire payments, you must have an active multi-currency account that's enabled for USD.

Webhook subscription: You should subscribe to the relevant webhooks in the ClearBank Portal to receive notifications about inbound payments.

Customer Accounts Transaction Completed webhook

Sent when a cross-border wire payment has been debited from or credited to your account. A transaction represents a ledger update for an account, and includes the minimum data necessary to support balance reconciliation.

How to simulate cross-border wire payments

Simulate inbound payments

In our EU simulation environment, you can simulate an inbound payment to your USD account using the POST /payments/cross-border/v2/simulations/inbound-payments endpoint. You can use this to fund your account before you test sending payments.

When you call the endpoint, we credit the settlementAmount to the simulation account with the iban you provide, and send you a Cross-border Inbound Payment Completed webhook and a Customer Accounts Transaction Completed webhook.

The simulated payment always comes from the same test debtor, named Simulated Debtor.

If the creditor account you specify doesn't exist in our simulation environment, isn't active, or isn't enabled for the settlementCurrency, you'll receive a 422 - Unprocessable Entity response.

Simulate an inbound cross-border wire payment endpoint

post/payments/cross-border/v2/simulations/inbound-payments

Simulate an inbound cross-border wire payment.

Parameters

  • Authorization string, header, Required

    Your API token, obtained from the ClearBank Portal.

  • DigitalSignature string, header, Required

    Signed hash of the body of the request. The hash is signed by your private key.

  • X-Request-Id string, header, Required

    A unique identifier for the request; valid for 24 hours, max length 83.

Request Payload (application/json)

  • iban string, Required

    The IBAN of your account to credit.

    Minimum length
    5
    Maximum length
    34
    Pattern
    [A-Z]{2,2}[0-9]{2,2}[a-zA-Z0-9]{1,30}
  • settlementAmount number, Required

    The amount to credit. Must be greater than zero, with no more than 2 decimal places.

    Minimum
    0
    Maximum
    1000000000
  • settlementCurrency string, Required

    The three-letter ISO 4217 currency code of the payment. Must match the currency of the account.

    Minimum length
    3
    Maximum length
    3
    Pattern
    ^[A-Z]{3}$
  • reference string

    A payment reference. This is sent in the remittance information of the Inbound Payment Completed webhook. The first 35 characters are also used as the instruction and end-to-end identifications.

    Maximum length
    140

Simulate outbound payments

To simulate an outbound cross-border wire payment, you can use the POST /payments/cross-border/v2/payments endpoint within our simulation environment. Valid requests to this endpoint in our simulation environment will trigger the associated outbound payment flow webhooks.

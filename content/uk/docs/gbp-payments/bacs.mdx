## Bacs

Bacs is the UK's high volume, low value payment system, offering two schemes: Bacs Direct Credit and Bacs Direct Debit. Bacs Direct Debit is the only UK payment scheme that supports pull payments, whereby a service user (the organisation collecting the payment) can collect funds from a payer's account under a Direct Debit Instruction.

### Bacs three-day cycle

Bacs operates on a three-day cycle: the **input day**, the **processing day**, and the **entry day**.

* **Day 1 - Input day**: a service user (the organisation originating the payments) submits a file of credit and/or debit payments to Bacs.
* **Day 2 - Processing day**: Bacs processes the file, and ClearBank receives details of the credits and debits due to be applied to your accounts.
* **Day 3 - Entry day**: payments are applied to the destination accounts. Settlement typically happens around 09:30 UK time on this day for direct Bacs participants.

![Image showing 3-day Bacs payment cycle](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-Days.svg "Bacs payment cycle")

A full list of direct Bacs participants can be found on <ExternalLink href="https://www.wearepay.uk/what-we-do/payment-systems/bacs-payment-system/payment-system-participant-list/">pay.uk's website</ExternalLink>.

#### Non-working days

Bacs does not operate on weekends or UK bank holidays. Only working days count towards the three-day cycle, so a file submitted on a Friday is processed on Monday and applied on Tuesday – five calendar days in total. You should allow for this when setting customer expectations around payment timings.

#### Notifying you of upcoming debits

When we receive a debit for one of your accounts on day 2 of the Bacs cycle, we'll notify you via the [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) webhook, and display the pending payment in the ClearBank Portal. This does not guarantee the payment will be taken, as our validation has not yet been applied. However, you may notify the account holder of the pending debit and, if necessary, ensure there are sufficient funds before the payment is applied on entry day.

#### Notifying you of upcoming credits

When we receive a credit for one of your accounts on day 2 of the Bacs cycle, we'll notify you via the [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) webhook, and display the pending payment in the ClearBank Portal.

Bacs credits can be recalled by the service user (the organisation sending the payment) or their Payment Service Provider (PSP) until 16:00 UK time on day 2 of the Bacs cycle. If this happens, we'll notify you via the [Bacs Direct Credit Recalled](#bacs-direct-credit-recalled-webhook) webhook, and the credit will not be applied to the account. We recommend waiting until after 16:00 UK time on day 2 before telling your customers about upcoming credits.

Before a credit is applied, it is screened. If it is flagged for review, you'll receive the [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Held](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-held-webhook) webhook, followed by a [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) webhook if the payment is released.

<Callout colour="blue">
  <strong>Note:</strong> You may receive Inbound Payment Created webhooks for accounts that are closed or do not exist, or for Direct Debits or Credits whose instruction has been cancelled. These payments should fail validation on entry day, and so you should not expect them to be applied to the account.
</Callout>

#### Payment validation and notification

On day 3 of the Bacs cycle, credit and debit payments are validated.

If validation is successful, the payments are applied to your accounts and you'll receive a [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) webhook for each payment.

Credits are applied first, from 01:00 UTC, followed by debits, from 03:00 UTC. Applying credits first helps ensure funds are available for any debits applied afterwards.

<Callout colour="blue">
  <strong>Note:</strong> When a Direct Debit is applied to a virtual account, ClearBank checks the balance of the underlying real account only. It is your responsibility to ensure the virtual account itself has sufficient funds. If it does not, you should return the debit using the <a href="#return-a-bacs-payment-for-a-virtual-account">virtual account returns endpoint</a>.
</Callout>

If validation fails, the payment is not applied to the destination account. Instead, it is posted to your Bacs suspense account and ClearBank automatically returns it to the originating bank through ARUCS (for credits) or ARUDD (for debits). You'll receive:

* a [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) webhook for the Bacs suspense account, and
* a [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook) or [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) webhook.

Returned transactions go through their own three-day Bacs cycle. Once complete, the returned funds are debited from or credited to your Bacs suspense account, and you'll receive a further [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) webhook.

You can also return a payment yourself after it has been applied. See [Returning a Bacs payment](#returning-a-bacs-payment).

### Webhook sequence summary

The table below summarises which webhooks to expect, and when, for inbound Bacs payments to your accounts. Use the `BacsTransactionId` to correlate the webhooks for the same payment.

| When | Event | Direct Credit webhook | Direct Debit webhook |
| ---- | ----- | --------------------- | -------------------- |
| Day 2 | Payment received from Bacs | [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) | [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) |
| Day 2 (until 16:00 UK time) | Credit recalled by the originator | [Bacs Direct Credit Recalled](#bacs-direct-credit-recalled-webhook) | N/A |
| Day 3 | Payment held for screening | [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Held](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-held-webhook) | N/A |
| Day 3 | Payment applied to the account | [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) | [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) |
| Day 3 | Payment failed validation and is returned automatically | [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) (suspense account) and [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook) | [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) (suspense account) and [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) |
| Day 3 or 4 | You return the payment using the returns endpoints | [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook) | [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) |
| Return cycle complete | Returned funds settle on your Bacs suspense account | [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) | [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) |

### Handling Direct Debit refunds and indemnity claims

If your customer notifies you they've been debited in error, they may be entitled to a refund under the <ExternalLink href="https://www.directdebit.co.uk/direct-debit-guarantee/">Direct Debit Guarantee</ExternalLink>.

Your institution should verify the reason for the payment error with your customer. If your institution agrees an error has taken place (more specifically, a deviation from the Bacs Direct Debit Rules), you should refund the customer from your own funds.

If your institution believes the error was made by the billing company (also called Service User - the entity that requested the debit) you can contact ClearBank to raise an indemnity claim against the service user. If ClearBank agrees with your analysis, we'll raise a claim to the Bacs Direct Debit scheme on your behalf, which will return the funds to the debtor's account 14 business days later.

The billing company may challenge your indemnity claim. If so, you'll need to examine the merits of the claim and decide whether to accept or reject their challenge. If you accept (or do nothing) then the challenge will succeed and the claim will be cancelled. It is then for you to decide if the error was yours, or if there was no error (in this case, if your customer has made an error then there is no error for you to resolve). You would then have the right to reverse the refund you gave to your customer.

## Returning a Bacs payment

You can return a Direct Credit or Direct Debit after it has been applied. Returns should be initiated as soon as possible, and no later than 15:30 UTC on the working day after settlement.

Debits and credits are returned using the same endpoints:

* For virtual accounts: [/v1/Accounts/{accountId}/Virtual/{virtualAccountId}/Transactions/Returns](#return-a-bacs-payment-for-a-virtual-account)
* For real accounts: [/v1/Accounts/{accountId}/Transactions/Returns](#return-a-bacs-payment-for-a-real-account)

### Return a Direct Credit payment

Use the endpoints below and specify the reason for return using a suitable [Direct Credit reason code](#arucs-direct-credit-reason-codes). You'll be notified with a [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook) on the day the return is created (day 3 or 4 of the original Bacs cycle). The funds will be moved from the customer's account to your Bacs suspense account on the same day, and returned to the originator on day 5 or 6. The message flow is shown here:

![Message flow for returning a Bacs Direct Credit using the returns endpoint](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-ReturnsDC.svg "Message flow: Return a Direct Credit payment")

### Return a Direct Debit payment

Use the endpoints below and specify the reason for return using a suitable [Direct Debit reason code](#arudd-direct-debit-reason-codes). You'll be notified with a [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) on the day the return is created (day 3 or 4 of the original Bacs cycle). The funds will be moved from your Bacs suspense account back to the customer's account on the same day, and returned to the service user on day 5 or 6. The message flow is shown here:

![Message flow for returning a Bacs Direct Debit using the returns endpoint](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-ReturnsDD.svg "Message flow: Return a Direct Debit payment")

## Bacs Direct Debit payment webhooks

The webhooks that relate to inbound Direct Debit payments have no direct association with an endpoint:

- [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook)
- [Transaction Settled webhook](#transaction-settled-webhook)
- [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) (this can occur automatically if ClearBank returns a payment, or in response to the Bacs returns endpoint)
- [Bacs Direct Debit Indemnity Claim Received Event webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-indemnity-claim-received-event-webhook)

These two webhooks relate to the origination of Direct Debit payments as they appear in the **service user's account**. You would see these if you were collecting the Direct Debit rather than paying it:
- [Bacs Debit Contra Received webhook](#bacs-debit-contra-received-webhook) - indicates funds received as a result of a Direct Debit file being processed. This is the bulk settlement.
- [Bacs Unpaid Direct Debit Received webhook](#bacs-unpaid-direct-debit-received-webhook) - indicates that a Direct Debit could not be paid.

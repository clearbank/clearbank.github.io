## Bacs

Bacs is the UK's high volume, low value payment service offering two schemes: Bacs Direct Credit and Bacs Direct Debit. Bacs Direct Debit is the only UK payment scheme offering debit payments, whereby creditors (the payment recipients) can retrieve funds from debtors' accounts.

### Bacs three-day cycle

Bacs operates on a three-day cycle: the **input day**, the **processing day**, and the **entry day**.

* **Input day**: a file of credit and debit payments is sent by debitors (the account holders initiating debit payments) to the Bacs scheme.
* **Processing day**: ClearBank receives details of debit payments to be made from the Bacs scheme.
* **Entry day**: Payment instructions are applied ('cleared') to the destination account. Settlement typically happens around 09:30 UK time on this day for direct Bacs participants.

![Image showing 3-day Bacs payment cycle](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-Days.svg "Bacs payment cycle")

A full list of direct Bacs participants can be found on <ExternalLink href="https://www.wearepay.uk/what-we-do/payment-systems/bacs-payment-system/payment-system-participant-list/">pay.uk's website</ExternalLink>.

#### Non-working days

The Bacs scheme does not operate on weekends or bank holidays; the aforementioned three-day cycle may cross over into the next working week if a payment is initiated in the lead-up to a weekend or bank holiday, potentially taking five full calendar days on a typical weekend.

#### Notifying you of upcoming debits

When we receive a debit instruction for one of your accounts on day 2 of the Bacs cycle, we'll notify you via the [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) webhook, and by displaying the pending payment in the ClearBank Portal. This is not a guarantee that payment will be taken since our validation has yet to be applied. However, this early notification allows you to notify the account owner of the pending debit and, if necessary, take remedial action before the payment is cleared on entry day.

#### Notifying you of upcoming credits

Similarly to debits, when we receive a credit instruction to one of your accounts on day 2 of the Bacs cycle, we'll notify you via the [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) webhook, and again by displaying the pending payment in the ClearBank Portal.

Bacs credit payments can be recalled by the service user (end customer) or their Payment Services Provider (PSP) until 4pm on day 2 of the Bacs cycle. We'll notify you if this happens via the [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Recalled](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-recalled-webhook) webhook, and the credit will not appear on the account. Delaying your notification of upcoming direct credits to your customers until _after_ 4pm UK time may therefore be wise, in case they are recalled.

<Callout colour="blue">
  <strong>Note:</strong> You may receive spurious Direct Credit and Debit notifications for accounts that have been closed, never existed, or for Direct Debits that have been cancelled. You should expect these to be resolved by our validation process on entry day before your accounts are credited or debited..
</Callout>

#### Payment validation and notification

On day 3 of the Bacs cycle, credit and debit payments are validated.

If validation is successful, the payments are applied to your accounts. You'll be notified of this via our [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) webhooks.

Credits are applied first, starting from 01:00 UTC, followed by debits, which are started from 03:00 UTC. Bacs participants apply credits first to help ensure funds are available for any subsequently applied debits.

<Callout colour="blue">
  <strong>Note:</strong> If a direct debit is being applied to a virtual account, it is your institution's responsibility to ensure that the virtual account and its underlying real account have sufficient funds to cover the debit.
</Callout>

If validation fails, the payments are not applied to your accounts, and the transaction will be posted to the Bacs Suspense account we've created for you. Failed payments each create their own webhook notifications:

For failed credits:
* [Bacs Direct Credit Failed](#bacs-direct-credit-failed-webhook)
* [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook)

For failed debits:
* [Bacs Direct Debit Failed](#bacs-direct-debit-failed-webhook)
* [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook)

Returned transactions are sent on their own new three day cycle. After three working days, returned funds are finally debited from or credited back to the suspense account. You'll receive a [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) webhook for each returned transaction accordingly.

### Returning credits and debits

#### Credit returns

You can issue a credit return using the our [Direct Credit Returns API](#direct-credit-returns-api). When you request a credit return, we'll transfer the funds from the customer's account to your Bacs Suspense account and raise the credit return with the Bacs Direct Credit scheme.

#### Debit returns

You can issue a debit return using the our [Direct Debit Returns API](#direct-debit-returns-api). When you request a debit return, we'll transfer the funds from your Bacs Suspense account to the customer's account and raise the debit return with the Bacs Direct Debit scheme.

We'll debit or credit the Bacs Suspense account as appropriate two working days later.

### Handling Direct Debit refunds and indemnity claims

If your customer notifies you they've been debited in error, they may be entitled to a refund under the <ExternalLink href="https://www.directdebit.co.uk/direct-debit-guarantee/">Direct Debit Guarantee</ExternalLink>  .

Your institution should verify the reason for the payment error with your customer. If your institution agrees an error has taken place (more specifically, a deviation from the Bacs Direct Debit Rules), you should refund the customer from your own funds.

If your institution believes the error was made by the billing company (also called Service User - the entity that requested the debit) you can contact ClearBank to raise an indemnity claim against the service user. If ClearBank agrees with your analysis, we'll raise a claim to the Bacs Direct Debit scheme on your behalf, which will return the funds to the debtor's account 14 business days later.

The billing company may challenge your indemnity claim. If so, you'll need to examine the merits of the claim and decide whether to accept or reject their challenge. If you accept (or do nothing) then the challenge will succeed and the claim will be cancelled. It is then for you to decide if the error was yours, or if there was no error (in this case, if your customer has made an error then there is no error for you to resolve). You would then have the right to reverse the refund you gave to your customer.

## Returning a Bacs payment

You can return a Direct Debit or Credit payment after it has been applied. Returns should be initiated as soon as possible, but can be initiated until 15:30 (UTC) the working day after settlement.

Debits and credits are returned using the same endpoints:

* For virtual accounts: [/v1/Accounts/{accountId}/Virtual/{virtualAccountId}/Transactions/Returns](#return-a-bacs-payment-for-a-virtual-account)
* For real accounts: [/v1/Accounts/{accountId}/Transactions/Returns](#return-a-bacs-payment-for-a-real-account)

### Return a Direct Credit payment

Use the endpoints below and specify the reason for return using a suitable [Direct Credit reason code](#arucs-direct-credit-reason-codes). You'll be notified with a [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook) on the day the return is created (day 3 or 4 after initiation).  The funds will be transferred to your Bacs suspense account on the same day and returned on Day 5 or 6. The message flow is shown here:

![Image showing a Inbound Bacs decision tree](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-ReturnsDC.svg "Message flow: Return a Direct Credit payment")

### Return a Direct Debit payment

Use the endpoints below and specify the reason for return using a suitable [Direct Debit reason code](#arudd-direct-debit-reason-codes). You'll be notified with a [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) on the day the return is created (day 3 or 4 after initiation). The funds will be transferred from your Bacs suspense account to the end-customer's account on the same day and returned on Day 5 or 6. The rest of the message flow is similar to returning a Direct Credit and is shown here:

## Bacs Direct Debit payment webhooks

The webhooks that relate to inbound Direct Debit payments have no direct association with an endpoint:

- [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook)
- [Transaction Settled webhook](#transaction-settled-webhook)
- [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) (this can occur automatically if ClearBank returns a payment, or in response to the Bacs returns endpoint)
- [Bacs Direct Debit Indemnity Claim Received Event webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-indemnity-claim-received-event-webhook)

These two webhooks relate to the origination of Direct Debit payments as they appear in the **service user's account**. You would see these if you were collecting the Direct Debit rather than paying it:
- [Bacs Debit Contra Received webhook](#bacs-debit-contra-received-webhook) - indicates funds received as a result of a Direct Debit file being processed. This is the bulk settlement.
- [Bacs Unpaid Direct Debit Received webhook](#bacs-unpaid-direct-debit-received-webhook) - indicates that a Direct Debit could not be paid.

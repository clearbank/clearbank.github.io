## Bacs

Bacs is the UK's high volume, low value payment service offering two schemes: Bacs Direct Credit and Bacs Direct Debit. Bacs Direct Debit is the only UK payment scheme offering debit payments, whereby creditors (the payment recipients) can retrieve funds from debtors' accounts.

### Bacs three-day cycle

Bacs operates on a three-day cycle: the **input day**, the **processing day**, and the **entry day**.

* **Input day**: a file of credit and debit payments is sent by debitors (the account holders initiating debit payments) to the Bacs scheme.
* **Processing day**: ClearBank receives details of debit payments to be made from the Bacs scheme.
* **Entry day**: Payment instructions are applied ('cleared') to the destination account. Settlement typically happens around 09:30 UK time on this day for direct Bacs participants.

![Image showing 3-day Bacs payment cycle](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-Days.svg "Bacs payment cycle")

A full list of direct Bacs participants can be found on <ExternalLink href="https://www.wearepay.uk/what-we-do/payment-systems/bacs-payment-system/payment-system-participant-list/">pay.uk's website</ExternalLink>.

#### Non-working days

The Bacs scheme does not operate on weekends or bank holidays; the aforementioned three-day cycle may cross over into the next working week if a payment is initiated in the lead-up to a weekend or bank holiday, potentially taking five full calendar days on a typical weekend.

#### Notifying you of upcoming debits

When we receive a debit instruction for one of your accounts on day 2 of the Bacs cycle, we'll notify you via the [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) webhook, and by displaying the pending payment in the ClearBank Portal. This is not a guarantee that payment will be taken since our validation has yet to be applied. However, this early notification allows you to notify the account owner of the pending debit and, if necessary, take remedial action before the payment is cleared on entry day.

#### Notifying you of upcoming credits

Similarly to debits, when we receive a credit instruction to one of your accounts on day 2 of the Bacs cycle, we'll notify you via the [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) webhook, and again by displaying the pending payment in the ClearBank Portal.

Bacs credit payments can be recalled by the service user (end customer) or their Payment Services Provider (PSP) until 4pm on day 2 of the Bacs cycle. We'll notify you if this happens via the [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Recalled](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-recalled-webhook) webhook. Delaying your notification of upcoming direct credits to your customers until _after_ 4pm UK time may therefore be wise, in case they are recalled.

<Callout colour="blue">
  Note: You may receive spurious Direct Credit and Debit notifications for accounts that have been closed, never existed, or for Direct Debits that have been cancelled. You should expect these to be resolved by our validation process on entry day before your accounts are credited or debited.
</Callout>



#### Applying credits and debits

On day 3 of the Bacs cycle, credit and debit payments are validated and, if validation succeeds, applied to your accounts.

Credits are applied first, starting from 01:00 UTC, followed by debits, which are started from 03:00 UTC. Applying credits first helps ensure funds are available for any subsequently applied debits.

Credits and debits that pass our validation checks will be applied to your accounts and you'll be notified via our [Transaction Settled](#transaction-settled-webhook) webhooks.

As inbound Bacs payments can either be credit or debit payments, the successful payment scenario can result in money moving either to or from the end-customer's account.
When a payment is successfully applied to an account, this message flow occurs:

![Image showing a successful Bacs payment](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-PaymentSuccess.svg "Successful Bacs payment")

If you subscribe to the [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) and [Bacs Direct Credit Inbound Payment Created](#bacs-direct-credit-inbound-payment-created-webhook) webhooks, then you can use the information to notify the account owner of a pending payment scheduled to occur on day 3. You can use the Bacs transaction ID to associate the webhooks that describe each payment. Note that credit payments can be recalled up to 4pm on the processing day by the originator; in this case you will be notified by a [Bacs Direct Credit Recalled webhook](#bacs-direct-credit-recalled-webhook) and the credit will not appear in the account.

Whether a payment is successfully applied to the end-customer's account is determined like this:

![Image showing a Inbound Bacs decision tree](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-DecisionTree.svg "Inbound Bacs decision tree")

If the payment cannot be applied to the account, ClearBank will automatically raise an ARUCS (Automated Return of Unapplied Credits Service) item  or an ARUDD (Automated Return of Unpaid Direct Debit) item. You will be notified with a [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook) or [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) webhook. Note that if a Direct Debit is paid from a virtual account, it is your responsibility to ensure that the account has sufficient funds. If it does not, use the returns endpoints below.

## Returning a Bacs payment

You can choose to return a payment that has been applied. Returns should be initiated straight away, but can be initiated until 15:30 (UTC) the working day after settlement.

**To return a Direct Credit** use the endpoints below and specify the reason for return using a [Direct Credit reason code](#arucs-direct-credit-reason-codes). ClearBank will notify you with a [Bacs Direct Credit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-credit-return-created-webhook) on the day the return is created (Day 3 or 4).  The funds will be transferred to your Bacs suspense account on the same day and returned on Day 5 or 6. The message flow is shown here:

![Image showing a Inbound Bacs decision tree](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-ReturnsDC.svg "Message flow: Return a Direct Credit")

**To return a Direct Debit** use the endpoints below and specify the reason for return using a [Direct Debit reason code](#arudd-direct-debit-reason-codes). ClearBank will notify you with a [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) on the day the return is created (Day 3 or 4). The funds will be transferred from your Bacs suspense account to the end-customer's account on the same day and returned on Day 5 or 6. The rest of the message flow is similar to returning a Direct Credit and is shown here:

![Image showing a Inbound Bacs decision tree](/assets/images/diagrams/uk/bacs/BacsOverview-ReturnsDD.svg "Message flow: Return a Direct Debit")

Use the endpoint below to return payments from a real account. To return payments from a virtual account, use: [/v1/Accounts/{accountId}/Virtual/{virtualAccountId}/Transactions/Returns](#return-bacs-payment-virtual-account)

## Bacs Direct Debit payment webhooks

The webhooks that relate to inbound Direct Debit payments have no direct association with an endpoint:

- [Bacs Direct Debit Inbound Payment Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-inbound-payment-created-webhook)
- [Transaction Settled webhook](#transaction-settled-webhook)
- [Bacs Direct Debit Return Created webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-return-created-webhook) (this can occur automatically if ClearBank returns a payment, or in response to the Bacs returns endpoint)
- [Bacs Direct Debit Indemnity Claim Received Event webhook](#bacs-direct-debit-indemnity-claim-received-event-webhook)

These two webhooks relate to the origination of Direct Debit payments as they appear in the **service user's account**. You would see these if you were collecting the Direct Debit rather than paying it:
- [Bacs Debit Contra Received webhook](#bacs-debit-contra-received-webhook) - indicates funds received as a result of a Direct Debit file being processed. This is the bulk settlement.
- [Bacs Unpaid Direct Debit Received webhook](#bacs-unpaid-direct-debit-received-webhook) - indicates that a Direct Debit could not be paid.

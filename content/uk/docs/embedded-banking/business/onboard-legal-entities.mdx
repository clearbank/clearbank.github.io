### Overview

Our legal entity onboarding endpoint lets you onboard new limited companies as business customers in a single request. Share key information about the company and its key people, and we'll handle the rest. When onboarding is complete, you'll receive a `CustomerId` that you can use to give the customer access to our products, such as [accounts](/uk/docs/embedded-banking/business/legal-entities-v2#compatible-account-types).

Every onboarding application includes our due diligence checks: Know Your Business (KYB) checks on the company, and Know Your Customer (KYC) checks on each of its key people. These checks can pass or fail, and we'll tell you the outcome by webhook. If the checks pass, we'll create the customer (a customer of type 'Legal Entity') and its key people for you. If they fail, no customer is created.

If you'd like to use this product, please contact your Client Director.

<Callout colour="blue">
  We can only onboard businesses that:<br /><br />
  • Are limited companies (Ltd).<br />
  • Are registered in the UK.<br />
  • Have UK tax residency.
</Callout>

### Usage

#### Initial request

To start an onboarding application, send us the limited company's business information and details of its key people, such as the company's controllers. We use this information to carry out our due diligence checks on the business and each key person.

When you submit an application, we'll validate your request and confirm with a **202 - Accepted** response containing the `applicationId` for this onboarding application.

<Callout colour="blue">
  You should retain the `applicationId` from our <strong>202 - Accepted</strong> response to identify the application in subsequent onboarding webhook events.
</Callout>

#### Providing additional information

As our checks progress, we'll request additional information about the customer from you via the [Legal Entity Additional Info Required](#legal-entity-customer-additional-info-required-webhook) webhook. This webhook contains an array of URLs (`RequiredActions[].url`) for you to share with the customer, who can then provide this information. For clarity, the type of additional information requested in each URL is specified in the `RequiredActions[].action` property.

Additional information we may request includes:

* Proof of ID and proof of address for key personnel, such as directors.
* Certain company documents.

<Callout colour="blue">
  You may receive more than one Legal Entity Additional Info Required webhook per application. For example, if a company director uploads a passport as proof of ID instead of a driving licence, we'll issue another webhook requesting their proof of address.
</Callout>

#### Successful onboarding

If the customer passes our due diligence checks, we'll create the new business customer automatically using the details in the application. You'll receive a [Legal Entity Application Successful](#legal-entity-application-successful-webhook) webhook containing a `CustomerId` for your records. You can use this `CustomerId` to give the customer access to our products.

To retrieve and manage the full details of a business customer and its associated persons, use the `CustomerId` with our [legal entity endpoints](/uk/docs/embedded-banking/business/legal-entities-v2).

#### Declined onboarding

If the customer does not pass our due diligence checks, you'll receive a [Legal Entity Application Declined](#legal-entity-customer-application-declined-webhook) webhook.  
The business customer will not be created, and the application will be terminated.

## Simulating onboarding webhooks

You can simulate successful, declined, and additional information onboarding webhooks in our simulation environment.  
To do this, call the [**POST /customer-due-diligence/v1/legal-entity/onboard**](#onboard-a-new-legal-entity-customer) endpoint with one of the `businessData.individuals[].name.surname` properties set to:

* `"pass"` to simulate checks passing, and receive a Legal Entity Application Successful webhook.
* `"fail"` to simulate checks failing for the overall business, and receive a Legal Entity Customer Application Declined webhook.
* `"info"` to simulate checks requiring additional information from that individual, and receive a Legal Entity Customer Additional Info Required webhook.

## Retail customer onboarding

Our retail customer onboarding endpoint lets you onboard new individuals as retail customers in a single request. Share key information about the customer, and we'll handle the rest. When onboarding is complete, you'll receive a `CustomerId` that you can use to give the customer access to our products, such as [accounts](/uk/docs/embedded-banking/retail/retail-customers#compatible-account-types).

Every onboarding application includes our Know Your Customer (KYC) due diligence checks. These checks can pass or fail, and we'll tell you the outcome by webhook. If the checks pass, we'll create the retail customer for you. If they fail, no customer is created. We may also need more information from the customer before we can complete our checks.

If you'd like access to this product, please speak to your Client Director.

<Callout colour="blue">
  We can only onboard customers who:<br /><br />
  • Currently live in the UK<br />
  • Have tax residency in the UK<br />
  • Do not have dual tax residency with another country<br />
  • Are not US nationals
</Callout>

## Simulating onboarding webhooks

You can simulate successful, declined and additional information onboarding outcomes - as well as additional information emails - in our simulation environment.  
To do this, call the [**POST /customer-due-diligence/v1/retail/onboard**](#onboard-a-new-retail-customer) endpoint with the `customerData.name.surname` property set to:

* `"pass"` to simulate checks passing, and receive a Retail Customer Application Successful webhook.
* `"fail"` to simulate checks failing, and receive a Retail Customer Application Declined webhook.
* `"info"` to simulate checks requiring additional information. To receive a Retail Customer Additional Info Required webhook instead of an email, ensure the `customerData.idvEmailsEnabled` property is set to `false`.

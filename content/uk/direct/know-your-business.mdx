### Overview

Our Know Your Business (KYB) endpoint makes it easy to meet your due diligence requirements when onboarding new Limited Companies. By sharing key company information, you can conduct Know Your Business checks on limited companies and receive the results via webhook.

For convenience, we'll also use the information provided in the KYC check to register the customer as a new Limited Company (a customer of type 'Legal Entity') and its key people if the checks succeed.

If you'd like to use this product, please contact your Client Director.

<Callout colour="blue">
  This service can only conduct KYB checks on businesses that:<br /><br />
  • Are limited companies (Ltd).<br />
  • Are registered in the UK.<br />
  • Have UK tax residency.
</Callout>

### Usage

#### Initial request

Our KYB endpoint consumes business information and details of key business personnel, such as company directors, for Limited Companies. We use this information to perform KYB checks on the business and KYC checks on each key person.

When you request a check using our KYB endpoint, we'll validate your request and confirm with a **202 - Accepted** response containing the `applicationId` for this KYB application.

<Callout colour="blue">
  You should retain the `applicationId` from our <strong>202 - Accepted</strong> response to identify the application in subsequent KYB webhook events.
</Callout>

#### Providing additional information

As our checks progress, we'll request additional information about the customer from you via the [Legal Entity Additional Info Required](#legal-entity-customer-additional-info-required-webhook) webhook. This webhook contains an array of URLs (`RequiredActions[].url`) for you to share with the customer, who can then provide this information. For clarity, the type of additional information requested in each URL is specified in the `RequiredActions[].action` property.

Additional information we may request includes:

* Proof of ID and proof of address for key personnel, such as directors.
* Certain company documents.

<Callout colour="blue">
  You may receive more than one Legal Entity Additional Info Required webhook per application. For example, if a company director uploads a passport as proof of ID instead of a driving licence, we'll issue another webhook requesting their proof of address.
</Callout>

#### Successful KYB check

If the customer passes our KYB checks, we'll create the new business customer automatically using the provided application details. You'll receive a [Legal Entity Application Successful](#legal-entity-application-successful-webhook) webhook containing a `CustomerId` for your records.

To retrieve and manage the full details of a business customer and its associated persons, please refer to our Legal Entity endpoints where you can retrieve these using the `CustomerId` provided by this service.

#### Declined KYB check

If the customer does not pass our KYB checks, you'll receive a [Legal Entity Application Declined](#legal-entity-customer-application-declined-webhook) webhook.  
The business customer will not be created, and the application will be terminated.

<Callout colour="blue">
  <strong>Note:</strong> We will sometimes require additional information from multiple people in the same application. You should expect to receive multiple 'Pending' webhooks (Customer.LegalEntity.AdditionalInfoRequired) for each person on the same KYB application.
</Callout>

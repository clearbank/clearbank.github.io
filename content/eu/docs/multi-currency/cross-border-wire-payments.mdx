Our cross-border wire payments service lets you send same-currency international payments from your ClearBank EU accounts. For example, you can send a USD payment from a USD account to a USD account held at another bank.

Cross-border wire payments are submitted in a single step. There's no FX quote to accept and no currency conversion.

If you're interested in using this service, please reach out to your Client Director.

## Payment process

### Send a payment

Payment requests should be submitted to our **POST /payments/cross-border/v2/payments** endpoint. We'll respond to a valid request with a **202 - Accepted** response, confirming that the payment has been accepted and queued for processing. No further action is needed from you to send the payment.

The **202 - Accepted** response contains a `paymentIdentification` array that maps the payment you submitted to the `paymentId` we've generated for it. You should retain the `paymentId` for your records.

### Request structure

The request body follows the ISO 20022 PAIN.001 message structure:

* `PaymentRequest` - the top-level message, identified by a `messageIdentification`.
  * `paymentInformation[]` - details that apply to the debit side of the payment, such as the `debtor`, `debtorAccount` and `requestedExecutionDate`.
    * `creditTransferTransactionInformation[]` - details of the individual payment, such as the `instructedAmount`, `creditor`, `creditorAccount` and `creditorAgent`.

Each request can contain one payment instruction, with one transaction.

### Duplicate payments

If you submit a payment with the same identifier as an existing payment, we'll respond with a **409 - Conflict** error.
